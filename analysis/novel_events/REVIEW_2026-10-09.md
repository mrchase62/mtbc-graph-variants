# Novel-event calling: where it stands (2026-10-09)

**Goal:** find structural events (deletions, insertions and others of 50 bp or
more) that an isolate has but its matched reference R does not. These are
events the graph cannot supply, because no panel genome carries them.

## 1. What was built, in standard terms

All of it works on the P2 alignment, which is the isolate's reads aligned with
bwa mem to its matched reference R.

| step | what it does | status |
|---|---|---|
| breakpoint caller | Finds places where reads are clipped (only part of each read aligns), split (the two parts align to different places) or paired abnormally. Clusters them, and types each cluster as a deletion, insertion, inversion, rearrangement or one-sided breakpoint. | v3b: quality-trims clips; a cluster must clip at one position and its clipped sequences must agree |
| local assembly | Assembles the reads around each candidate with SPAdes and aligns the contigs to R, to type and size the event | typed candidates and strong one-sided breakpoints only, at most 40 windows per isolate |
| depth scan | Looks for 100-bp windows with too many or too few reads (copy-number change) | GC-corrected; **does not work on real reads** (section 4) |
| dysgu, delly | Established short-read SV callers, run in P2 | used for comparison and in the combination |
| uncallable mask | Marks sequence where short reads cannot be placed: R's own repeats (50-bp stretches that occur more than once; short tandem repeats), plus the H37Rv repeat mask carried over to R | new; 9-10% of each R |

**Test sets:**
- Phase A: 40 isolates with simulated reads.
- Phase B: 62 real Illumina isolates. Each has a genome assembly from Marin et al., which gives the true events.

| Phase B group | isolates | role |
|---|---:|---|
| novel | 20 | not in the panel; the main test |
| setE_polished | 17 | an independent assembly set; its truth is less reliable |
| in_panel | 25 | the isolate's own genome is in the panel, so there should be almost no events; this is the false-call check |

## 2. Most real structural differences are in repeats

A true event counts as **uncallable** when either of its breakpoints is within
50 bp of R's uncallable mask.

| set | true events | in uncallable sequence |
|---|---:|---:|
| Phase A (simulated) | 993 | 831 (84%) |
| Phase B novel | 461 | 387 (84%) |
| Phase B setE_polished | 663 | 575 (87%) |

- **Both mask sources agree:**
  - In the novel group, 299 of the 387 are flagged by the measured repeats *and* by the H37Rv mask.
  - Removing the 50-bp margin changes 387 to 383.
- **This is the main finding.** Between closely related *M. tuberculosis*
  genomes, about 5 in 6 structural differences fall in IS6110 copies, PE/PPE
  genes and tandem repeats. Short reads cannot reliably place them there.
- **One refinement to consider:** 47 of the novel group's uncallable events
  are IS6110 copies present in R but missing from the isolate. Their
  breakpoints are the element's own ends, so the rule flags them. The empty
  site's flanks are unique, though, so these may be callable from the flanks.

## 3. Results, all sequence vs callable sequence only

**Typed recall** is the fraction of true events found with the right type.
**Precision** is the fraction of typed calls that match a true event.

| caller | Phase A all | Phase A callable | novel all | novel callable | setE all | setE callable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| dysgu PASS | 0.51 / 0.65 | 0.88 / 0.74 | 0.28 / 0.14 | 0.66 / 0.07 | 0.31 / 0.40 | 0.64 / 0.28 |
| delly PASS | 0.26 / 0.49 | 0.32 / 0.81 | 0.22 / 0.05 | 0.11 / 0.00 | 0.17 / 0.40 | 0.15 / 0.14 |
| our caller + assembly | 0.38 / 0.67 | 0.57 / 0.71 | 0.34 / 0.58 | 0.49 / 0.50 | 0.23 / 0.60 | 0.49 / 0.47 |
| ours + dysgu PASS | 0.60 / 0.66 | **0.90 / 0.73** | 0.47 / 0.24 | **0.74 / 0.11** | 0.42 / 0.48 | **0.77 / 0.34** |

Each cell is typed recall / precision. Callable sets: Phase A 162 events, novel
74, setE 88; small numbers, especially for the novel group.

- **In callable sequence, recall on real reads is close to the simulations**
  (0.74-0.77 against 0.90). The callers can see these events.
- **Precision on real reads is the weak point.** In the novel group:
  - dysgu PASS makes 722 calls in callable sequence, and 7% of them match a true event;
  - our caller + assembly makes 76, and 50% of them do.

## 4. False calls in the 25 in-panel controls

These are typed calls that match no true event (20 true events in total).

| caller | per isolate, all sequence | per isolate, callable | median per isolate, callable | worst isolate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| our caller + assembly | 36.2 | 31.3 | **1** | 424 |
| dysgu PASS | 157.0 | 135.6 | 4 | 505 |
| delly PASS | 966.8 | 826.3 | 3 | 6,586 |
| depth scan | 616.7 | 439.0 | 48 | 3,259 |

- **Most controls are clean, but about 7 are not.** Those are mostly the "N"
  series, whose panel genomes are GCF_0228702xx. In them:
  - our caller makes 33-463 calls;
  - dysgu makes 300-580;
  - delly makes 1,000-7,000.

  The reads and the panel assembly of "the same" isolate disagree there. Possible
  causes are a different culture, assembly errors, or poor read quality. This
  has **not been investigated**, and some of these "false" calls may be real
  differences.
- **The depth scan is not usable on real reads.** Even GC-corrected, it makes a
  median of 48 false calls per control in callable sequence, and its precision
  is 0.004-0.10. It stays out of the combination.

## 6. What this means

1. **With short reads, novel-event calling can assess only about 1 in 6 real
   structural differences.** The rest are in repeats, and they are now flagged
   uncallable rather than called or missed silently. For those, the matched
   reference from the graph is the main source of SVs.
2. **Inside callable sequence, detection is good** (about 0.75 typed recall).
   **Precision is the problem:** dysgu adds recall but brings many false calls.
   Our caller + assembly is the most precise single component, at about 0.5.
3. **The depth scan should be dropped** in its current form.
4. **Some in-panel controls disagree with their own panel assembly.** That
   should be understood before the controls are used to set any filter.

## 7. Next steps

- **GRIDSS: tested and dropped.** On 5 QC'd isolates (callable sequence, 17
  true events) it found 59% of breakpoints but typed only 18% of events, and
  made 2-3 typed false calls in each control. Scripts kept in `gridss/`.
- **Read QC adopted in test** (fastp, rasusa to 100x, minimum 60x, TB-Profiler
  mixed removed; kraken2 not yet run): dysgu PASS precision 0.29 to 0.52,
  control false calls 0-7 to 0-2, recall about the same. A full Phase B rerun
  with QC (about 90 billing-hours) awaits approval.
- **Not pursued:** breseq (too slow, misses events without saying why); a
  lower bwa seed length.
- **Possible:**
  - make IS6110 copy presence/absence callable from the unique flanks
    (section 2);
  - look at the 7 noisy controls;
  - drop the depth scan.
